About

Nick Benoit is a New Orleans bassist on upright and electric, performing around the city as a live, studio, and session player.

His ear for harmony goes back to the trumpet, which he played from fourth grade through high school, rotating through every trumpet part in jazz band. He picked up bass guitar at 15 playing rock and metal, then turned toward jazz after moving to New Orleans in 2009, when he also took up the upright.

At Tulane University he earned a B.S. in Psychology and a B.F.A. in Jazz Studies, studying with Jesse McBride, Jim Markway, John Doheny, and Adam Benjamin of the fusion band Kneebody. He spent a semester at the Conservatorium van Amsterdam and completed the coursework for an M.A. in ethnomusicology specializing in the music of New Orleans.

Bandleaders call him for the slap and keep calling because he listens: Nick plays what the song needs, so your music still sounds like yours.
